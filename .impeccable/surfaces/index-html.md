# Clay Me · whole app (redesign)

Scope: every kid-facing screen of index.html. Mode: Operate (kids doing a task, often with a clinician beside them).
Audience/job: kids 4–12, many with language disorder or pre-readers, noticing body → feeling → saying it; in session, at home alone, or with a parent.
Constraints: keep all features, content, clay photos and the made buddy; Australian English; Reduce Motion; one screen per task; phone first, big screens and mouse too.
Unresolved: which secondary features move under More.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Clay Me is a picture book the child makes as they go. It refuses the pastel-card kids' app (cream ground, soft rounded tiles, emoji icons everywhere).

OWN-WORLD: Crisp white page. Hand-painted cut-paper shapes with visible brush grain in a full palette: tomato, marigold, leaf, cobalt, violet, bubblegum, ink navy for text. Each feeling and each area owns one paper colour. Cards are irregular cut-paper silhouettes, not rounded rectangles. Chunky friendly display face for words kids read; Atkinson Hyperlegible for everything else.

STORY: The child sees their buddy on the page, taps a big paper shape, and does one thing per screen. Their choices come back as a sentence in their own colours.

FIRST VIEWPORT (phone home): top strip with "hi Name" and today's weather as a small painted-paper sky chip. Buddy large, centre-left, on a painted paper ground patch. Three big cut-paper doors stacked right/below: My body (leaf), Feelings (marigold), Say it (cobalt). Bottom tab bar, 5 tabs: Home, My body, Feelings, Say it, More. Desktop: same, tabs as a left rail, doors in a row.

FORM: painted-paper collage picture book; #6 of my ordered list; seed:39e98da7e16ab861.

SIGNATURE: on tap, a paper shape lifts (tilt + paper shadow) and its colour washes across the top band of the next screen, so every screen wears the colour you chose.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
