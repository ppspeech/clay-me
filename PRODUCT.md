## Platform

web

## Users
- Kids in session with Pei-Pei (speech pathologist), sharing one screen with the clinician alongside. Many have language disorder; some are pre-readers.
- Kids at home on a family device, on their own.
- Parents using it with their child at home.

## Product Purpose
Clay Me helps kids notice what's happening in their body, find a word for the feeling, and say it to someone. It is bottom-up (body first, label second) and trauma-informed: nothing is required, "not sure" is always an answer, and kids can stop any time. Success is a child who can point to where they feel it and leave with a sentence they can use ("I felt sad when … because … Can we …?").

## Positioning
Built by a speech pathologist for kids with language difficulties: picture-led, minimal reading, sentences modelled from the child's own choices, and the child's own made avatar used throughout.

## Operating Context
Used in therapy sessions (tablet or laptop, clinician beside the child) and at home (phones, tablets, family laptops, sometimes mouse-only desktops). Free on GitHub Pages (ppspeech.github.io/clay-me) so other clinicians and families can use it. Each kid's progress is stored only in their own browser, per named kid; "just playing" saves nothing.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Current features to keep: who's-here landing (per-kid names); home with "weather inside" picker, swimming pond fish, floating buddy; check-in (squish a feeling blob, colour/size/shape/where); body scan (tap a body part, slide on a big/small × nice/not-nice square, glows); face/voice/hands/legs/body clue cards with "maybe…" feeling suggestions; How I look; Colours and colour mixing; Spot a feeling; Animals; Feelings (body-first lines, icebergs); Pond; Stories; Say it (feeling → what happened → how it felt inside → I-statement, own words, read aloud, record own voice, save); What helps (helper picker + "you said…" bubble); make-my-own buddy builder (folk/crayon style, inclusive heads, hair, hijab/patka, mobility aids, pets, scenes). Single self-contained index.html (~8.5 MB, images embedded). Respects Reduce Motion. Australian English.

Parked: grown-up hub, Mandarin version, recorded real-voice clips.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Clay Me. Clay-figure photography (Kai, Ruby, Mei, Theo and clue/scene photos, made in Gemini) and the folk-style made buddy are existing assets. Kid-facing voice: very few words, warm, playful, never clinical.

## Evidence on Hand
~430 embedded clay images (faces, clue cards, scenes, animals, textures). No testimonials or outcome data; do not invent any.

## Product Principles
1. Body first, words second, choice always.
2. Fewer words, bigger pictures: a pre-reader can use every screen.
3. Everything on one screen; minimal scrolling and navigation.
4. Nothing is wrong, nothing is forced: "not sure" and "not now" are always there.
5. The child's own buddy and own words are reflected back to them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Kids with language disorder and pre-readers (picture + read-aloud). Inclusive avatars (skin tones, hijab, patka, hearing aids, AAC, wheelchair, etc.). Mouse-only, touch, phone and big screens. Reduce Motion honoured.
